    Coached By Ernest: Wix Website Design & Lead Generation

    UX/UI & Product Design|Life coaching & professional development

    Building a coaching website designed to turn visitor interest into consultation inquiries

    ~3×Booking and consultation requests
    3 monthsLead period

    Project snapshot:

    • Project Type: Coaching website
    • Platform: Wix
    • Build Time: 36 days
    • Lead Period: 3 months
    • Live website: https://www.coachedbyernest.com/

    Project Overview

    Coached By Ernest is a life-coaching business focused on helping professionals turn intention into achievement through coaching, accountability, and structured support. The website presents the founder, explains the coaching offer, and gives prospective clients ways to explore services and request a consultation.

    I designed and developed the website from the ground up using Wix. My responsibilities covered the full website experience: UI/UX design, personal branding presentation, content arrangement, booking setup, contact-form optimization, newsletter integration, e-commerce functionality, calls to action, SEO, and mobile-responsive design. The website build took 36 days.

    The Challenge

    The project needed to give a coaching business a clear and credible online presence while making it easier for interested visitors to take the next step. A coaching website has to do more than describe services: it needs to communicate the coach’s approach, help visitors recognize which service is relevant to them, and make it straightforward to request a conversation.

    The business also needed a stronger path from website interest to direct inquiries. The project therefore focused on the presentation of the founder and services, alongside practical contact and booking routes.

    Research & Planning

    I planned the website around the questions a prospective coaching client may have: who is the coach, what kind of support is offered, why might coaching be useful, what services are available, and how can someone get in touch or book a session?

    The page structure visible in the supplied homepage screenshot supports this journey. It begins with a clear value proposition, introduces the coach, explains why a visitor might need a life coach, presents the available services, and then provides testimonials and newsletter/contact opportunities.

    Design Approach

    The visual direction combines a calm, professional foundation with a warm accent color. The screenshot shows deep blue-green headings, a muted green founder-introduction section, light neutral backgrounds, and orange calls to action. This creates visual contrast while keeping the overall presentation approachable.

    I gave the founder a prominent role in the homepage through a large hero portrait and a dedicated introduction section. The service descriptions are grouped into 1:1 Coaching, Group Coaching, and Webinars, so visitors can scan the available options. Testimonials are presented as a separate trust-building section, and calls to action appear at key points in the page.

    The design was also planned for mobile responsiveness so visitors could access the content and contact routes across screen sizes.

    Development Process

    I implemented the website in Wix and built the page structure around the planned visitor journey. The development scope included the website layout, responsive presentation, booking system setup, contact form optimization, and newsletter integration.

    I also worked on SEO as part of the website project. The supplied information confirms SEO was included.

    Key Features

    Founder-focused hero and introduction.

    The homepage leads with the coach’s message and portrait, helping visitors connect the service with the person providing it.

    Coaching services.

    Dedicated descriptions for 1:1 Coaching, Group Coaching, and Webinars help visitors understand the different ways to work with the coach.

    Booking call to action.

    The visible Book Today action and booking setup provide a direct route for visitors who are ready to request a session.

    Contact form and inquiry routes.

    Contact-form optimization supports visitors who prefer to send an inquiry rather than book immediately.

    Testimonials.

    A dedicated client-testimonial section adds social proof and helps prospective clients understand the experience of working with the coach.

    Newsletter subscription.

    The newsletter form gives visitors another way to stay connected if they are not ready to book at the moment.

    Responsive layout and SEO.

    The project included mobile-responsive design and SEO work as part of the overall website implementation.

    E-commerce functionality.

    The website also includes an e-commerce component, extending the site beyond service information and inquiry generation.

    Lead Generation Strategy

    The lead-generation work focused on reducing friction between interest and action. I worked on the placement and clarity of calls to action, the booking journey, and the contact form, while the wider website also included e-commerce functionality. The founder introduction, service explanations, and testimonials support visitor understanding and confidence.

    Based on the client-provided project information, booking or consultation requests and phone/email inquiries increased to approximately three times the previous level over a three-month period following the website work.

    Final Outcome

    The delivered website provides Coached By Ernest with a complete Wix-based coaching presence, including founder-led messaging, service information, e-commerce functionality, booking, contact forms, newsletter signup, testimonials, SEO work, and responsive layouts.

    The reported business outcome was approximately 3× more booking/consultation requests and phone/email inquiries over three months compared with the previous level. The website design and lead-generation work were completed as a 36-day build.

    Key Takeaways

    This project reinforced that lead generation is closely connected to how clearly a website explains its offer and how easily visitors can act on their interest. For a personal coaching brand, the founder’s presence, service clarity, testimonials, and accessible booking/contact options all contribute to the decision-making journey.

    It also showed the value of treating website design and lead-generation functionality as parts of the same project. In future projects, I would establish a clear baseline for inquiries before launch and use consistent tracking for bookings, forms, phone calls, and email inquiries so that changes can be measured more precisely.

    Leaders On The Fly: Website Design & Development

    UX/UI & Product Design|Fly fishing & leadership retreats

    Making the journey from website visit to booking inquiry clearer

    22 daysDesign and development
    45 daysSEO

    Project snapshot:

    • Project: Website redesign
    • Platform: WordPress + Elementor
    • Build: 22 days
    • Seo: 45 days

    Project Overview

    Leaders On The Fly brings together guided fly-fishing experiences and leadership-focused business retreats. Its programs use time outdoors and on the river as a setting for learning, team connection, reflection, and personal development. The business highlights Broken Bow, Oklahoma as its primary location, with guided opportunities available throughout the year.

    I worked on the website design, WordPress development, and SEO. The website was built with WordPress and Elementor. The website redesign and development phase took 22 days, followed by 45 days of SEO work.

    The Challenge

    The main business problem was not simply getting people to visit the website. Visitors were arriving, but the site was not turning enough of that interest into booking inquiries. The business needed a clearer path from discovering an experience to understanding it and taking the next step.

    The challenge was therefore to make the offer easier to understand, improve the presentation of trust-building content such as testimonials, and make the booking or inquiry action more visible and straightforward.

    Research & Planning

    I approached the project around the questions a prospective guest or business decision-maker would need answered: What does Leaders On The Fly offer? Is it a guided fishing trip or a leadership retreat? Where does it take place? Who is it for? And how can someone ask a question or make a booking?

    The page structure brings the core offer into view early, then gives visitors more context through service descriptions, supporting photography, testimonials, destination information, and a contact call to action. This creates a more understandable sequence for people who may be considering different types of experiences.

    Design Approach

    The visual direction uses the brand’s established navy-and-gold identity. The logo and color treatment help connect the website to the outdoor, premium-experience positioning of the business. I used large photography to communicate the setting and the human side of the experiences, while keeping the written content close to the relevant imagery.

    The homepage is organized into distinct content areas: an introductory hero, an overview of the business, guided fishing information, leadership retreat information, customer testimonials, retreat destinations, and a closing contact section. The intent was to make the different services easier to scan rather than presenting them as one long, undifferentiated message.

    I also revised the testimonial presentation and the calls to action. The goal was to make social proof easier to notice and reduce friction for visitors who were ready to ask about availability or booking.

    Development Process

    I developed the website in WordPress using Elementor. This provided a visual page-building workflow for arranging the homepage sections and refining their layout and content hierarchy.

    Alongside the design and development work, I carried out an SEO phase over 45 days. The visible website content establishes relevant subject and location themes, including guided fly fishing, leadership and team retreats, Broken Bow, Oklahoma, and year-round guided opportunities. These themes provide a foundation for communicating the business’s services and geographic relevance to search visitors.

    Key Website Features

    Clear introductory message.

    The homepage introduces the connection between business, learning, and fly fishing, helping visitors understand that the company offers more than a conventional fishing trip.

    Guided fly-fishing information.

    A dedicated content area explains the guided fishing experience and gives prospective guests more context about who the trips may suit.

    Leadership retreat presentation.

    A separate section explains the business-retreat and leadership-training offer, helping business audiences recognize the relevance of the experience.

    Reworked testimonials.

    Customer testimonials are presented as a visible trust-building part of the page, supporting visitors who want reassurance before making an inquiry.

    Retreat destination cards.

    Destination cards highlight locations such as Broken Bow, Beavers Bend State Park, and the Mountain Fork River, making the experience more tangible.

    More accessible booking and contact actions.

    The calls to action were adjusted to make the next step easier to find and understand, reducing the effort required to move from browsing to contacting the business.

    Challenges & Solutions

    The central challenge was the gap between website visits and booking inquiries. A visitor could be interested in the experience but still hesitate if the offer, supporting proof, or next action was not immediately clear.

    I addressed this by improving the content hierarchy, separating the main service propositions, bringing testimonials into the page experience, and making the contact and booking calls to action easier to identify. The design decisions were intended to support visitor confidence and make the inquiry journey more direct.

    Final Outcome

    The delivered result is a WordPress website built with Elementor that presents Leaders On The Fly’s guided fishing experiences and leadership retreats in a more structured way. The homepage brings together service explanations, location context, photography, testimonials, destination highlights, and contact prompts in one visitor journey.

    The project included 22 days of website design and development and a further 45 days dedicated to SEO work. The redesign focused on making the business offer clearer and making the booking inquiry path easier to follow.

    Key Takeaways

    This project reinforced the importance of designing around the decision a visitor needs to make, not just around the visual appearance of a homepage. For a business with more than one core service, the page needs to explain each offer clearly while still communicating one coherent brand.

    It also highlighted the role of trust and clarity in a booking journey. Testimonials, relevant photography, location information, and clear calls to action each help answer a different question a prospective customer may have. In future projects, I would pair these design improvements with conversion tracking and Search Console reporting from the outset so that changes in inquiries, organic visibility, and user behavior can be evaluated with reliable data.

    • Platform: WordPress
    • Page builder: Elementor
    • Website design & development: 22 days
    • SEO period: 45 days
    • Website: https://leadersonthefly.com/

    • Project Type: Corporate website

    Project Overview

    By The Marina Ventures is an investment- and business-ventures-focused company based in Nashville, Tennessee. The project was to create a corporate website that could introduce the business, communicate its identity, and present the founder’s professional profile in a clear and considered way.

    I designed and developed the website from scratch over a 15-day period. My role covered the website’s structure, visual design, WordPress implementation, page layouts, responsive presentation, and the overall user experience. The build used WordPress, Elementor, and the Hello Theme.

    The Challenge

    The brief called for more than a basic company homepage. The website needed to establish a professional digital presence for the business while giving the founder’s profile an important place within the overall experience. It also needed to make the company’s identity and relevant content easy to explore.

    The central design task was to balance two connected stories: the company as a business and the founder as a professional figure associated with it. The site therefore needed a coherent corporate presentation without making the experience feel crowded or difficult to navigate.

    Research & Planning

    I used three inputs to shape the direction: my own research, reference websites supplied by the client, and the client’s preferences for color, typography, and layout. Rather than treating any one input as the sole direction, I brought them together to guide the website’s structure and visual presentation.

    The content structure was planned around the main things a visitor might want to understand or explore: the founder’s profile, company-related information, images, related reading, and social channels. The navigation visible on the supplied homepage screenshot reflects this content grouping through Home, Profile, Images, and Related Reading.

    This structure keeps the primary content areas distinct and gives visitors straightforward ways to move between them.

    Design Approach

    The visual direction is restrained and editorial. The supplied homepage screenshot shows a warm taupe background, light typography, generous open space, and a prominent founder portrait. These elements create a calm, understated presentation that keeps attention on the person and the company introduction.

    I translated the client’s preferences and the visual references into a consistent page treatment. The introductory copy is centered and kept concise, with a clear Profile call to action directly below it. The portrait provides a strong visual anchor, while the Related Reading and Images areas are presented as separate destinations rather than competing with the introduction.

    The navigation and footer continue the same warm, neutral visual language. The intention was to maintain consistency across the site while giving each content area enough space to be understood.

    Development Process

    I built the website from scratch in WordPress using the Elementor page builder and Hello Theme. This combination provided the foundation for implementing the page structure and customizing the visual layout to match the agreed direction.

    The implementation focused on translating the planned content hierarchy into usable website sections, applying the selected typography and color direction, and organizing the navigation and calls to action. The project was completed within 15 days.

    Key Website Features

    Founder introduction and Profile CTA.

    The homepage introduces the founder and provides a direct Profile action, giving visitors a clear route to learn more about the person behind the business.

    Corporate introduction.

    The introductory content explains the company’s establishment and broad business focus, helping visitors understand the organization at a glance.

    Primary navigation.

    Home, Profile, Images, and Related Reading create a simple route through the site’s main content areas.

    Image section.

    A dedicated Images area gives visual material its own place in the website structure.

    Related Reading.

    A separate Related Reading section provides a clear destination for supporting written content.

    Social media links.

    The social media area connects the website experience with the company’s external social presence.

    Challenges & Solutions

    One of the main design considerations was giving the founder’s profile enough prominence while preserving the broader corporate purpose of the website. I addressed this through the content order: a concise company introduction, a prominent founder image, and a direct Profile call to action.

    Another consideration was keeping the site’s content areas easy to distinguish. The navigation and separate Images and Related Reading sections provide a straightforward structure without overloading the homepage with competing information.

    The design direction also needed to reflect the client’s references and preferences while remaining consistent across the site. I used the same restrained color and typography approach through the introductory area, navigation, content sections, and footer.

    Final Outcome

    The final deliverable is a corporate website created from scratch for By The Marina Ventures. It brings the company introduction and founder profile together with dedicated areas for images, related reading, and social links. The navigation and Profile call to action give visitors clear ways to explore the available information.

    The delivered website reflects the agreed design inputs—developer research, client references, and client preferences—and was implemented in WordPress with Elementor and the Hello Theme within 15 days.

    Key Takeaways

    This project reinforced the value of planning a corporate website around the information visitors need, especially when the founder’s professional profile is an important part of the company’s identity. The company and founder need to feel connected, while each still needs a clear place in the content structure.

    It also demonstrated how research, client references, and direct client preferences can be combined into a focused visual direction. A restrained layout can support a professional presentation when typography, spacing, imagery, and navigation are handled consistently.

    For future projects, I would define responsive review checkpoints and content approval stages early, then document the final behavior across screen sizes. This makes the design process easier to validate and gives the finished work a clearer quality-assurance record.

    Darul Iman Educational Institution: WordPress Security & Malware Cleanup

    Web Development & Product Engineering|Education

    Malware Removal, Spam Link Cleanup & Website Recovery

    1 monthTimeline
    WordPressPlatform

    Project snapshot:

    • Project Type: WordPress security
    • Platform: WordPress
    • Tools: Wordfence, cPanel, SSH
    • Timeline: 1 month
    • Live website: https://darulimanedu.com/

    Project Overview

    Darul Iman is an educational institution with an online presence at darulimanedu.com. This project focused on investigating and cleaning a WordPress website affected by malware, spam links, and redirects to suspicious websites.

    I handled the website security investigation and cleanup process. The work included scanning the website, identifying infected files, removing malicious PHP and JavaScript code, cleaning the database, reviewing administrator accounts, checking WordPress core files, themes and plugins, and applying security-hardening measures.

    The project took approximately one month, including testing and the agreed cleanup guarantee period. The objective was to remove the identified malicious content, address the visible security issues, and verify the website’s behavior after cleanup.

    The Security Challenge

    The website was affected by several connected security symptoms: malware, spam links, and redirects to suspicious external websites. These issues can undermine visitor confidence and make a legitimate institutional website appear unsafe or unreliable.

    The task was not limited to removing a visible spam link. The website needed a broader review of its files, database, administrator accounts, WordPress installation, themes, and plugins so that suspicious code and unauthorized access points could be investigated as part of the same cleanup effort.

    Security Audit & Investigation

    I began with a security scan and a review of the WordPress installation. Wordfence was used as part of the malware scanning and security-audit workflow. I also used the hosting file environment and database tools to inspect website components beyond the front-end pages.

    The investigation covered the areas identified in the project scope: website files, PHP and JavaScript code, database content, administrator accounts, WordPress core, themes, and plugins. The purpose was to look for suspicious changes and determine which parts required cleanup or further review.

    Malware Detection & Analysis

    The cleanup process included identifying infected or suspicious files and reviewing malicious PHP and JavaScript code. The presence of spam links and suspicious redirects also made database review an important part of the investigation.

    I used cPanel File Manager and SSH to work with website files, and phpMyAdmin to inspect and clean relevant database content.

    WordPress Malware Removal & Cleanup

    After identifying suspicious components, I carried out the cleanup across the website’s file and database layers. This included removing malicious PHP and JavaScript code, cleaning spam links and suspicious database content, and reviewing unknown administrator accounts.

    I also checked the WordPress core, installed themes, and plugins as part of the cleanup process. This broader review was intended to ensure the work addressed more than the visible symptoms and included the main components that make up the WordPress site.

    Security Improvements & Hardening

    Following the cleanup, I worked through the security-hardening tasks included in the project. These covered reviewing administrator access, checking the WordPress installation and its extensions, and using Wordfence as part of the security workflow.

    Testing & Website Recovery

    Testing was included in the one-month project timeline. After the cleanup and security review, I checked the website to assess whether the reported issues malicious content, spam links, and suspicious redirects were still appearing during the verification process.
